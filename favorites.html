My favorite hobbies

Some of my favorite hobbies include playing soccer, collecting hot wheels, playing video games, and listening to music. These are things I enjoy doing, especially soccer. Soccer is my passion and it's something I've been playing since I've been 4 years old. I started collecting hot wheels a few years ago and I really enjoy collecting cars I find cool or finding rare items.

My favorite movies

My top 5 movies include The Godfather, The Godfather part II, Hacksaw Ridge, Casino, and The Dark Knight. Most of these movies were shown to me by my dad and I loved them when I first watched them. The Godfather movies to me are masterpieces and Hacksaw Ridge is a very emotional movie to me. I really like the Dark Knight because of Batman and an honorable mention would be the Spider Man Brand New day or Avengers Infinity War and Endgame. As well as Spider-Man(Tobey Maguire).

My favorite music

My favorite artist is Daddy Yankee, but right now I'm listening to a lot of Rauw Alejandro, Bad Bunny, and Feid.

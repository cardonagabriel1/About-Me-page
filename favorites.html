Favorites:

Welcome to my personal favorites showcase! On this page, you can explore the movies, music artists, and food dishes that I enjoy the most in my daily life. Click on any picture in the gallery below to open a larger gallery view.

 

Categorized Favorites

1. Favorite Movies

  • The Godfather & The Godfather Part II – Absolute cinematic masterpieces that I love watching with my dad. Check out film reviews on IMDb.
  • Hacksaw Ridge – A deeply emotional war film that hits hard every time.
  • The Dark Knight – The best Batman superhero film with an unforgettable Joker performance.

2. Favorite Music

  • Daddy Yankee – My absolute favorite artist and the king of reggaeton. Listen to his top tracks on Spotify.
  • Rauw Alejandro & Feid – Two of my current favorite Latin artists to listen to right now.
  • The Marías & Mac DeMarco – Great indie artists I listen to when chilling. Read more about indie music on Pitchfork.

3. Favorite Foods

  • Pozole – Traditional, comforting, and packed with flavor.
  • Quesabirrias – Cheesy, savory, and amazing with consomé dipping broth.
  • Carne Asada Fries – The ultimate comfort food with melted cheese and guacamole.
